# EcoMind AI — Campus Energy Policy (Demo Policy Framework)

1. Purpose and Scope
   - This policy establishes guidelines for energy conservation, demand management, and sustainable building operation across all campus facilities.
   - It applies to Academic Blocks, Computer Laboratories, Libraries, Hostels, and Administrative Facilities.

2. Target Energy Standards
   - Target a 10% overall reduction in non-essential power consumption across all campus facilities during peak hours.
   - Maintain power factor above 0.95 to optimize grid power quality and reduce billing penalties.

3. Monitoring & Reporting Protocol
   - Building managers must review weekly energy analytics reports and anomaly alerts provided by EcoMind AI.
   - Any persistent anomaly exceeding 2.0 standard deviations above baseline requires physical audit within 48 hours.

4. Governance & Human Oversight
   - Recommendations produced by EcoMind AI are advisory tools for facility managers and sustainability coordinators.
   - Facility managers retain final operational authority over HVAC, lighting, and power distribution adjustments.
