# EcoMind AI — Campus Energy Saving Guidelines (Demo)

1. HVAC & Climate Control Optimization
   - Maintain air conditioning temperatures between 24°C and 26°C across all academic blocks and administrative offices.
   - Schedule automated shut-off or set-back modes during non-operational hours (20:00 to 07:00).
   - Ensure windows and doors remain closed while HVAC systems are active to minimize thermal loss.

2. Computing Laboratories & IT Infrastructure
   - Enable automated power-saving or sleep states on lab workstations after 15 minutes of inactivity.
   - Power down high-performance computing equipment and auxiliary monitors overnight and during weekends.
   - Consolidate active server loads where practical to improve power usage effectiveness (PUE).

3. Lighting & Auxiliary Equipment
   - Utilize daylight harvesting in libraries and classrooms during daytime peak sunlight hours.
   - Upgrade fixture lamps to high-efficiency LEDs with occupancy sensor control in corridors and restrooms.
   - Turn off non-essential hallway lighting, promotional displays, and laboratory apparatus when unneeded.

4. Demand Peak Management
   - Shift non-time-critical laboratory equipment cycles and maintenance tasks away from peak hours (10:00 to 16:00).
   - Coordinate large events to prevent simultaneous peak HVAC and heavy equipment startup spikes across multiple buildings.
