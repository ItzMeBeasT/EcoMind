# EcoMind AI — Core Problem Statement

## The Challenge

Educational institutions consume substantial electricity daily across multiple facilities including classrooms, computer laboratories, libraries, student hostels, and administrative blocks.

However, campus administrators and facility managers face critical operational challenges:
1. **Lack of Automated Intelligence**: Facility managers lack automated data tools to identify unexpected energy spikes, equipment left running overnight, or inefficient HVAC setpoints.
2. **Unpredictable Energy Demand**: Fluctuating weather conditions and irregular student class schedules make manual energy load planning inaccurate.
3. **Delayed Reaction to Waste**: Energy waste is typically discovered weeks later when utility bills arrive, rather than in real time.
4. **Lack of Actionable Recommendations**: Raw meter data does not automatically explain *why* consumption spiked or *what steps* should be taken to mitigate waste.

---

## Target Audience & Stakeholders

- **Campus Facility Managers**: Need real-time anomaly alerts, building-level load comparisons, and concrete operational advisories.
- **Sustainability Coordinators**: Require data-driven metrics on energy usage patterns and estimated emissions reductions.
- **Institutional Administrators**: Need quantifiable energy cost projections and clear policy compliance reporting.
