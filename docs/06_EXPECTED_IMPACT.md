# EcoMind AI — Expected Impact & Scenario Analysis

## Impact Framework

EcoMind AI provides quantifiable scenario-based impact modeling to estimate energy, cost, and emissions savings under user-defined efficiency reduction assumptions.

> **Important Disclosure**: All impact metrics are clearly labeled as **hypothetical scenario estimates** calculated from configurable assumptions, not measured institutional campus results.

---

## Scenario Simulation Methodology

### Baseline Assumptions
- **Electricity Grid Emission Factor**: `0.82 kg CO2e / kWh` (typical grid electricity baseline)
- **Assumed Electricity Tariff**: `INR 8.0 / kWh` (configurable commercial tariff estimate)
- **Target Operational Reduction**: `5.0%` (achievable through off-hour standby shutdown, peak load shifting, and HVAC setpoint adjustments)

---

## Simulated Campus Results (5% Reduction Scenario)

| Impact Category | Baseline (Full Dataset) | 5% Reduction Scenario | Savings Achieved |

## Non-Financial Sustainability Benefits
1. **Behavioral Awareness**: Interactive dashboard visualizations encourage energy-saving practices among campus staff.
2. **Proactive Maintenance**: Early detection of unusual building baseload prevents equipment overheating and catastrophic failures.
