# EcoMind AI — Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment

## Primary SDG Alignment

### **SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy**
- **Target 7.3**: "By 2030, double the global rate of improvement in energy efficiency."
- **EcoMind AI Impact**: Directly addresses energy efficiency within institutional facilities by detecting baseload energy waste, optimizing HVAC operating setpoints, and shifting peak electrical loads.

---

## Secondary SDG Alignments

### **SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities**
- **Target 11.6**: Reduce the adverse per capita environmental impact of campus communities through smart energy infrastructure management.

### **SDG 13 — Climate Action**
- **Target 13.3**: Improve education and institutional capacity on climate change mitigation by providing transparent, data-driven estimates of avoided CO2 emissions.
