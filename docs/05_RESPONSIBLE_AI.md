# EcoMind AI — Responsible AI & Governance

EcoMind AI is designed in accordance with core Responsible AI principles to ensure ethical, transparent, and safe deployment.

---

## Core Responsible AI Pillars

### 1. Privacy & Data Protection
- **Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**: The smart meter dataset records aggregate building electricity consumption without monitoring individual students, faculty, or staff.
- **Facility-Level Aggregation**: Data points are collected strictly per building facility (e.g., Academic Block, Computer Lab).

### 2. Fairness & Non-Discrimination
- **Objective Physical Metrics**: Machine learning inputs consist exclusively of environmental metrics (temperature, occupancy counts, equipment counts, time of day).
- **No Demographic Inputs**: The system does not evaluate or categorize individuals based on protected attributes.

### 3. Transparency & Explainability
- **Non-Causal Advisory Terminology**: Insights explicitly state "possible contributing factor" rather than claiming proven causality.
- **Interpretable Models**: RandomForest feature importance and IsolationForest decision scores provide clear technical explanations for system outputs.

### 4. Human Oversight
- **Advisory Decision Support**: Recommendations generated by EcoMind AI are advisory tools. Campus facility managers retain full operational control and final authority over physical HVAC, lighting, and power distribution systems.

### 5. Synthetic Data Limitation Disclosure
- **Synthetic Prototype Data**: The operational dataset is synthetic demo data created for project demonstration purposes. No claims of real institutional deployment results are made.

### 6. Model Limitations & AI Guardrails
- **Probabilistic Estimates**: ML energy forecasts represent statistical predictions and should not be treated as guaranteed physical outcomes.
- **Generative AI Grounding**: The conversational agent ("Ask EcoMind") decouples numerical analysis from natural language generation to eliminate hallucinations.
